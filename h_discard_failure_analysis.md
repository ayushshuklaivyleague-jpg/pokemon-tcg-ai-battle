# `H_DISCARD` Failure Analysis & Structural Post-Mortem

**Document Timestamp**: 2026-08-30  
**Target Investigation**: Root-cause analysis of `H_DISCARD` dormancy and evaluation of discard failure hypotheses (`DF-01` to `DF-04`).

---

## 1. Overview of Failure Hypothesis

Prior research in `discard_hypotheses.md` and `discard_failure_clusters.md` posited:
- In `DISCARD_ENERGY` states, V4 preferentially sacrificed scarce Draw Supporters (`Waitress` at $5,000$ points, `Lillie` at $5,000$ points) over `Basic {W} Energy` (scored at $30,000$ points).
- Supposedly, this occurred across 182 decisions in 300 matches.

---

## 2. In-Depth Technical Post-Mortem

### Finding 1: Engine Option Architecture in `DISCARD_ENERGY`
When the Pokémon TCG engine invokes `SelectContext.DISCARD_ENERGY`:
- The engine prompts the player to pay an energy cost (e.g. retreating an Active Pokémon or paying Kyogre's attack requirement).
- Every legal option generated by the engine specifies:
  - `type`: `OptionType.ENERGY` ($6$)
  - `area`: `AreaType.ACTIVE` ($4$) or `AreaType.BENCH` ($5$)
  - `index`: Pokémon in-play index
  - `energyIndex`: $0, 1, 2, \dots$ (the index into the Pokémon's attached `energyCards` array)
- The attached cards on Pokémon in this deck are exclusively `Basic {W} Energy` (Card ID $3$).
- The engine **never** exposes cards in the player's hand (`AreaType.HAND`) as legal options during `DISCARD_ENERGY`.

### Finding 2: Root Cause of Prior Counterfactual Artifact
Why did prior offline corpus analysis identify 182 states where Supporters were discarded?
- In `main.py`, `v4_card_from_option(obs, option)` contains the following loop:
  ```python
  for field in ("hand", "deck", "discard", "prize", "bench"):
      cards = safe_list(safe_get(player, field, []))
      if index < len(cards) and cards[index] is not None:
          return cards[index]
  ```
- In `DISCARD_ENERGY`, `option.index == 0` (referring to Active Pokémon slot $0$).
- Because `v4_card_from_option` did not inspect `option.energyIndex` and checked `"hand"` first, it evaluated `player.hand[0]`.
- If `player.hand[0]` happened to be `Waitress` or `Lillie's Determination`, the offline analyzer incorrectly logged that `Waitress` or `Lillie` was the discard option!
- In reality, the option was always attached `Basic {W} Energy`.

## 3. Deck Card Catalog & Absence of Hand Discard Mechanics

An exhaustive scan of all cards in `deck.csv` confirms:
1. **`Basic {W} Energy` (35 copies)**: Attached energy resource.
2. **`Kyogre`, `Snover`, `Abomasnow`, `Mega Abomasnow ex`**: Pokémon line.
3. **`Mega Signal`**: Searches deck for Mega Pokémon.
4. **`Maximum Belt`**: Tool card boosting attack damage.
5. **`Cyrano`**: Supporter searching for 3 Pokémon.
6. **`Waitress`**: Supporter drawing cards.
7. **`Lillie's Determination`**: Supporter shuffling and drawing cards.

There are **zero** cards in the deck that require discarding cards from hand (such as `Ultra Ball`, `Professor's Research`, or `Quick Ball`). Therefore, hand cards are never discarded in this deck archetype.

---

## 4. Synthesis & Architectural Invariant

- `H_DISCARD` is structurally inert because its precondition (simultaneous presence of Basic Energy and Draw Supporters in a discard selection) cannot occur in this deck archetype.
- No overrides were triggered across 3,346 decisions in 200 matches.
- The 52.50% win rate observed in the benchmark is consistent with self-play noise around the 50.0% theoretical control baseline.
- **Decision**: Reject `H_DISCARD` promotion. Keep production `main.py` frozen.
